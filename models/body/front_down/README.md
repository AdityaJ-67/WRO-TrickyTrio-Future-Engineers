# Front Lower Chassis Section

<img src="front_down_image.png" width="400">

The lower front section of the body. Carries the Ackermann steering assembly and the
steering servo, and forms the front floor of the car.

This is the section that takes the load from the steering linkage, so it is the
stiffest part of the body. Flex here would change the steering geometry under load,
which would make the wheel angles differ from what the servo was commanded.
