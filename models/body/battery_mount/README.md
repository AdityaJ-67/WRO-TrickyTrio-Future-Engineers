# Battery Mount

<img src="battery_holder_image.png" width="400">

Holds the 2S LiPo pack low and central in the chassis.

**Why position matters.** The battery is one of the heaviest single items on the robot,
so where it sits determines how weight is distributed between the axles. Weight over
the driven rear wheels gives them grip; too much weight forward makes them slip under
acceleration. Mounting it low also keeps the centre of gravity down, which reduces how
much the car leans when cornering hard.

It is also the part most likely to need removing mid session, since the pack has to
come out to charge, so the mount is designed for the battery to be swapped without
dismantling anything else.
