# Front Upper Chassis Section

<img src="front_top_image.png" width="400">

The upper front section of the body. Closes over the steering assembly and provides the
mounting surface for the camera mount.

Splitting the body into upper and lower halves means the steering linkage stays
accessible for adjustment without reprinting anything, which mattered a great deal while
we were still tuning the Ackermann geometry.
