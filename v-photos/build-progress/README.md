# Build Progress Photos

Photographs taken while the robot was being built, rather than of the finished thing.

## Why this folder exists

Documentation is judged partly on whether the engineering process is visible. A photo
of a finished robot proves the robot exists. A photo of the third version of a bracket
next to the first two proves that a design process happened, and that is a different
and more valuable claim.

Failed attempts are worth more here than successful ones.

## What is worth photographing

<!-- TODO: add photos here. Anything from this list is worth keeping: -->

- The chassis at each stage, especially before it worked
- Prints that failed, warped or did not fit, next to the version that did
- The first wiring attempt, loose wires and all, alongside the finished PCB
- Soldering in progress
- The steering linkage during assembly
- The robot on the bench with test equipment attached
- Anything that broke
- The team actually working, not posed

## Naming

Dated names sort themselves into order and make the sequence obvious:

```
2026-03-14-first-chassis-print.jpg
2026-03-14-chassis-warped.jpg
2026-04-02-steering-assembly-v2.jpg
```

A one line caption for each, in this README, turns a folder of photos into a record of
what happened.
